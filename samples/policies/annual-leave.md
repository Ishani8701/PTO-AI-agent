# Annual Leave Policy

## Global

Full-time employees accrue paid annual leave monthly. Baseline accrual is
1.25 days per month (15 days per year). Leave must be requested at least
2 weeks in advance. Carryover is capped at 5 days; excess is forfeited on
January 31.

## US

US employees receive 15 days of PTO per year. PTO covers both vacation and
personal days — there is no separate personal-day allotment. Carryover is
capped at 5 days.

## IN

India employees receive 20 days of Earned Leave (EL) per year, credited at
1.67 days per month. EL may be carried over up to 30 days in total accrual;
excess is encashable at year end. Employees additionally receive 12 days
of Casual/Sick Leave (CL/SL) credited on January 1, which does not carry
over.

## DE

German employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per year, above the
statutory minimum of 20 days. Leave is credited on January 1. Unused leave
carries over only until March 31 of the following year, after which it is
forfeited unless carryover was denied for operational reasons.

## UK

UK employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per year, which includes
the 8 UK bank holidays. This matches the statutory minimum under the
Working Time Regulations. Carryover is capped at 5 days.
