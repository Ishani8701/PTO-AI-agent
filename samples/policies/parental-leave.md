# Parental Leave Policy

## Global

New parents are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the
birth or adoption of a child. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the
qualifying event.

## US

US employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave, aligned with FMLA
job-protected leave. Birthing parents receive an additional 6 weeks of
paid medical leave for recovery.

## IN

India employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave under the
Maternity Benefit Act, and 2 weeks of paid paternity leave. Adoption
leave follows the same rules based on primary caregiver status.

## DE

German parents are entitled to up to 3 years of job-protected Elternzeit
per child. Elternzeit itself is unpaid; parents typically claim Elterngeld
from the state for up to 14 months shared between parents. Acme tops this
up with 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver,
taken within the first 12 months.

## UK

UK employees are eligible for up to 52 weeks of Statutory Maternity Leave
(SML), with Statutory Maternity Pay for up to 39 weeks. Fathers and
partners receive 2 weeks of Statutory Paternity Leave. Acme enhances SMP
to full pay for the first 16 weeks of maternity leave and full pay for
the 2 weeks of paternity leave.
