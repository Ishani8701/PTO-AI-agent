# Sick Leave Policy

## Global

Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year, separate from annual
leave. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than 3
consecutive working days.

## US

US employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. State-level laws (e.g.
California) may add statutory minimums, which HR reconciles automatically.

## IN

India employees are covered by the 12 days of Casual/Sick Leave (CL/SL)
described in the annual leave policy. A medical certificate is required
for sick absences of more than 2 consecutive working days.

## DE

German employees have no cap on sick leave. Under the Continued
Remuneration Act, employers pay full salary for up to 6 weeks (42 calendar
days) per illness. Employees must notify their manager on the first day
of illness and submit a medical certificate
(Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung) by the 4th day at the latest.

## UK

UK employees receive 10 paid sick days per year as company sick pay, in
addition to Statutory Sick Pay (SSP). A GP "fit note" is required for
sickness absences of more than 7 calendar days.
